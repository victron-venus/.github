# Licensing decision and scope

The owner confirmed rights and authorized MIT publication for original source,
tests and documentation on 2026-10-16. The root [LICENSE](../LICENSE) publishes
that grant. [Third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) retain imported CI
helper attribution, external-font terms, project names and marks. A public link
or repository-admin role alone does not license newly imported material; future
contributions must retain provenance and compatible terms.

This licensing decision resolves the missing original-source grant. It does not
establish every other OpenSSF criterion, deployment behavior, or certification.
