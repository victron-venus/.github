Documentation

Everything needed to go from a stock Cerbo GX to the full open stack — and the standards that keep the codebase maintainable.

Where to start

  1. Skim the architecture — five layers, two buses (D-Bus on-device, MQTT everywhere else). Two minutes well spent before installing anything.
  2. Follow the install guide for your hardware layer. Each step is independent; adopt only what you need.
  3. Dive into Venus OS integration when writing your own service — service naming, MQTT topics, PackageManager installs.
  4. Before your first PR, read contributing and the CI standards every repo here follows.
