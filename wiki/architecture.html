02 · Architecture

How the system fits together

One rule drives the whole design: Venus OS speaks D-Bus, everything else speaks MQTT. Every project here is either a translator at that boundary or a consumer on one side of it.

The five layers

1 · Hardware & sensing

ESP32s, Tasmota plugs, CT clamps, Emporia Vue submeters. Firmware publishes raw readings. esphome-jbd-bms-mqttdbus-esphome-grid-sensor

2 · D-Bus services

Translators running on the GX: MQTT→D-Bus for batteries, HTTP→D-Bus for PV meters, WebSocket→D-Bus for AC loads. Third-party hardware becomes native Victron devices. dbus-mqtt-batterydbus-tasmota-pvdbus-emporia-vue

3 · Control & policy

The only layer allowed to write to the inverter. inverter-control decides setpoints; venus-os-governance gates every command against SOC limits and policies; dbus-event-log records both. inverter-controlvenus-os-governancedbus-event-log

4 · MQTT distribution

State leaves the GX over MQTT topics. fastapi-mqtt-gateway adds REST/WebSocket access for clients that can't speak MQTT natively. fastapi-mqtt-gateway

5 · Consumption

Dashboards (Go binary on-GX, Python/Docker, Vue SPA, Tauri desktop), Home Assistant, observability stacks, and the LangGraph forecaster feeding pre-charge hints back into control. inverter-dashboard-gosolar-forecast-langgraphvenus-os-observability

Why two buses instead of one

D-Bus is how Venus OS itself models the world — battery, PV inverter, grid meter all appear as com.victronenergy.* services, so the built-in ESS logic treats third-party hardware as first-class. That's non-negotiable for control.

But D-Bus is local-only. Dashboards on a laptop, Home Assistant, Grafana, and forecasters live elsewhere — they subscribe to MQTT mirrors of the same state. The split keeps safety-critical paths on the bus the OS controls, and convenience paths on the protocol every tool already has a client for.

Control path vs telemetry path

Cross-cutting concerns

  • Forecasting loop: solar-forecast-langgraph reads MQTT history plus OpenMeteo, then asks inverter-control to pre-charge before cloudy periods — closing the loop through the same governed write path.
  • AI access: mcp-venus-os exposes reads freely but routes writes through the same safety constraints as any other client.
  • Testing: integration-tests runs the bridge services against a real broker and simulated D-Bus peers in CI, so a firmware change upstream can't silently break the chain.
